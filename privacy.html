This Privacy Policy explains how Dodge The Enemies (“we”, “us”), a free game for Android developed by DoDgeClan, handles information when you play. By using the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: the nickname and password you choose when you create an account.
  • Game data: progress, score, coins, XP, level, missions, achievements, cosmetics and leaderboard results.
  • Social features: your friends list, game rooms, and the messages, emojis, photos and voice messages you send in chat. Photos and voice are shared only if you choose to use those features.

The game does not ask for your real name, home address or precise location.

2. How we use it

  • To run the game, save your progress and let you sign in on your devices.
  • To show leaderboards and enable friends and chat.
  • To keep the game safe, fix bugs and improve it.
  • To answer your questions and support requests.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Other players can see your nickname, rank and game results, and chat content is visible to the people you send it to. We may use hosting providers to run the game’s servers; they process data only on our behalf. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please choose a strong password that you do not use anywhere else.

5. Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it together with your progress, friends and messages (see below).

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will remove it.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email us using the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed, so please review this page periodically.

9. Contact us

Questions or suggestions? Email mrdoxer.kz@gmail.com.

Delete your account

To delete your account and all data linked to it, send us an email from the address you use with us, with your in-game nickname. We will delete your account, progress, friends and messages within 30 days.
